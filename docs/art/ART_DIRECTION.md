# Art direction: painted-miniature combat

Reference images (concept art supplied by the project owner; mood and treatment only, not layout specs,
and their garbled text is not content):

- `combat_target.png`: the target look for tactical combat.
- `charsheet_target.png`: portrait and character-sheet treatment.

## Combat

- **Low-key lighting.** Dark, desaturated stone; warm, motivated pools of torch and brazier light;
  deep shadow between them. No flat, even daylight fill. Night and dungeon fights are the default mood;
  daylight streets are dimmer and moodier than before.
- **Figures read as painted metal tabletop miniatures** (28 mm wargame figures): pewter and bronze
  metal under worn, hand-painted colour, darker recesses, bright edge highlights, a small flocked base.
  Crisp silhouettes and dynamic, readable combat poses. Matte paint, not glossy plastic or clay.
- **Heavy architecture.** Thick walls with broad capstones, chunky masonry, grim palette. Buildings
  read as a dungeon or fortified ruin, not a bright toy town.
- **Active-character close-up.** A framed panel showing the active character in their surroundings,
  lit by the same scene, beside the battlefield, alongside the stats.
- **UI.** Keep the Gold Box command line and roster, framed in the darker metal/rope/leather skin.

## Portraits and party figures

- Painted portraits in an ornate dark frame, lit to match, consistent with the miniature style:
  the portrait is the painted face of the same figure that fights on the board.
- Character sheet may keep the original Gold Box text layout (STR..CHA column, LEVEL, AC/HP, THAC0,
  DAMAGE, ENCUMBRANCE, MOVEMENT, STATUS) rendered with a premium metal-lettered treatment.

## Don't

- No TSR/SSI logos, copyright lines or trademarks.
- No bright saturated clay/toy look; no evenly lit scenes.
